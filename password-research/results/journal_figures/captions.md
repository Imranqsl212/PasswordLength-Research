## Figure 1

Theoretical support under independent uniform draws from four specified ASCII alphabets. N=94 excludes space. No empirical sample denominator or source-password entropy is implied.

## Figure 2

All primary groups 6–16: 173,904,254 units of released Account Mass and 57,238,505 Distinct Anonymous Bins. G50 orders each known-length group separately. Medians use the stated distinct-bin or mass weights; these are perturbed historical spectra, not population estimates.

## Figure 3

All eleven primary source-length groups, each divided by its own released Account Mass. Length 15 is dashed. Curves stop at the available positive-bin support; lines connect evaluated budgets. The oracle presumes length and the popularity order are known. No operational guessing or account recovery was measured.

## Figure 4

Left: empirical released conditional concentration versus mathematical min(K/Nᴸ,1), matched only by nominal length; source encoding and generation method are unknown. Right: entropy from four explicit injective uniform generators, checked using 16,800 frozen feature records. Equal local ASCII length permits different known generator entropy. This is not a matched human/random attack trial.

Six fixed exploratory models, eleven held-length folds. Each threshold and boundary-tie fraction uses only the other ten primary groups and is frozen before application to the held spectrum. Outcome thresholds differ across folds; weighted log loss is an aggregate diagnostic, not prospective user/source prediction. The held denominators total 57,238,505 Distinct Anonymous Bins. ## Figure 6

Complete exploratory grid: seven minimum lengths × six budgets = 42 cells. Each curve divides selected pooled-oracle mass by the released mass of all supplied lengths meeting its threshold. The original ≥16/top10,000 cell is retained. Sharp tie bounds and distinct-bin denominators are exported separately. Threshold sensitivity does not identify individual strings, cause, original population fractions or real attack success.

## Figure 7

Independent uniform draws with replacement from 7,776 EFF words, joined by one space. Left: 1,500 frozen phrases; markers are medians of three replicate medians and bars are medians of replicate 10th–90th length quantiles, not confidence intervals. Right: exact convolution counts conditional on known word count and joined length; dashed lines are unconditional entropy. No ordinary human-sentence entropy is estimated.

## Figure 8

36 cells, one warmup and five timed calls per operation/cell. Upper/left panels show matching-verification medians and descriptive quartile spans. Levels: PBKDF2 100k/300k/600k; Argon2id 19/32/64 MiB, t=2/2/3, p=1; bcrypt cost 8/10/12. Lower-right retains all five sorted hash times for Argon2id 32 MiB, 8 bytes, including 299.644 ms. Sorted times are recovered exactly from n=5 quantiles; temporal order was lost. Unequal parameter settings and one host do not rank security or attacker throughput.

## Figure 9

Separately analyzed historical spectra at six fixed common budgets. Denominators: LinkedIn full 174,292,189; Yahoo all 69,301,337 units of released Account Mass. They are not pooled. Differences describe overall concentration under their own frequency orders. Yahoo has no length covariate, so this cannot replicate the length association or establish a temporal trend.
